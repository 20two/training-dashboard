Coaching notes
Win

Engine held. Avg 144 / max 171 over 30 min with no blow-up. Pace improved late (1:57 round at minute 24). Aerobic base is rebuilding — exactly the goal.

Fix

Push-ups are a muscular failure, not a cardio one. HR during push-ups (145) was the lowest of the three movements, yet they took the most time (10:58). You're resting local muscle, not catching breath. The fix is a rep scheme, not a bigger engine.

Fix

The middle valley cost you a round. Rounds 5 & 6 both ran 3:38 vs 2:27–2:50 earlier. Holding 2:50 there banks ~90s — the difference between 286 and finishing round 11.

Watch

Box jumps are erratic (34s to 88s per round). Step-down is right for the knee — but make it a metronome at ~50s, not a variable.

Data

Tempus showed the deadlift at 225 lbs; actual was 135 — a stale manual preset, not a bug (weights aren't measured). Worth correcting in-app so history stays honest.
