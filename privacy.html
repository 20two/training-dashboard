Privacy Policy

TrainingCoach · Effective July 30, 2026

TrainingCoach is a personal training-readiness app. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled. In short: your health data stays on your device, and the developer never receives it.

Health data

With your permission, TrainingCoach reads the following data from Apple Health (HealthKit) on your device: sleep duration, heart rate variability (HRV), and resting heart rate. It uses this data solely to calculate a daily training-readiness assessment and coaching recommendation for you.

TrainingCoach only reads this data. It does not write to Apple Health.

Where your data goes

All processing happens entirely on your device. TrainingCoach does not transmit your health data, or any other personal data, to the developer, to any server, or to any third party. The developer has no access to your health data and does not collect, store, sell, or share it.

Your health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never shared with data brokers or advertising networks.

Your control

You grant Health access when you first use the app, and you can review or revoke it at any time in the Health app under Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → TrainingCoach. Deleting the app removes any data it stored on your device.

Children

TrainingCoach is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
