Coach's gameplan
The one rule
The bike is the dial. Pick a calorie pace you can repeat three times and hold it. This is the McGhee lesson applied — you sandbagged that middle and had a gear left at the end. Even beats fast. Moderate and repeatable on rounds 1–2, then empty the tank on the round-3 bike.

Before you start

  • Connect the Echo bike over Bluetooth so Tempus counts your calories — that's your one hard data stream today.
  • Rope out a few reps to find your rhythm and let the Achilles / calves warm in before round 1.
  • Tap promptly at each transition so the rope → sit-up → bike splits stay clean (splits are tap-driven, not auto-counted).

How to run each piece

75 Jump rope — any style

Smooth and continuous, unbroken or 25/25/25. Land soft and midfoot. This is the only spot your limiters live — if the Achilles or calves start talking, break to smaller sets rather than pushing through. No sprinting; it's not where the workout is won.

25 AbMat sit-ups

Steady, unbroken, anchor your feet. Treat this as your relative rest between the two engine pieces — don't spike your heart rate here.

25 cal Echo bike

The lever. Moderate and repeatable rounds 1–2, then empty the tank on round 3. This is where you spend whatever you've got left — and the one segment with real counted data.
